pwnbox flake

The pwnbox NixOS + home-manager flake.

Install on an existing NixOS machine:

1. Download and extract the archive.
2. Enter the flake directory:
       cd pwnbox
3. Run the installer:
       ./install.sh switch

The installer detects your user, hostname, time zone and hardware, creates
hosts/<host>/ for the machine, then activates the configuration. Garbage
collection is opt-in with --gc.

Manual: https://pwnbox.one/docs/
